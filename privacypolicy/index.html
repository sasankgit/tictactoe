Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Toe is a game published by Neverlos. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Information handled by the app

The game does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other profile information. Game moves and scores are used to run the current game on your device; the app does not send them to Neverlos or store them on a Neverlos server.

Advertising and third-party services

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner and interstitial advertisements. The SDK may automatically collect and share information with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on the SDK version, device settings, and how the service is used, this can include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • advertising ID and other device or app identifiers;
  • interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps, and ad views; and
  • diagnostic and performance information about the app and SDK.

Google handles this information under its own policies. Information sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Google Mobile Ads data disclosure. You can manage advertising personalization through Google My Ad Center and your Android device’s advertising settings.

How Neverlos uses information

Neverlos does not receive or maintain the advertising information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The game’s board and scores are used only to provide gameplay on your device. We do not use them for profiling, analytics, or advertising.

Retention and deletion

The app does not maintain user accounts or store gameplay data on Neverlos servers, so there is no Neverlos account or server-stored game data to delete. Advertising data collected by Google is subject to Google’s retention and deletion practices. Use the Google privacy controls linked above to manage information associated with Google services.

Children’s privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been provided directly to Neverlos in connection with this app, contact us at neverlos49@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or its data practices change. The current version will be published at this page, with its effective date updated above.

Contact
